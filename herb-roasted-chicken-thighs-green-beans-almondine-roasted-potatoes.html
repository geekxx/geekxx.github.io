Herb-Roasted Chicken Thighs, Green Beans Almondine, Roasted Potatoes

Juicy bone-in chicken thighs roasted with herbs, served with roasted potatoes and green beans almondine.

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1/4 cup sliced almonds
  • 2 tbsp butter

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Season chicken thighs with thyme, rosemary, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Toss potatoes with oil, salt, and pepper and place in the roasting pan alongside chicken. Roast for 35–40 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F and potatoes are fork-tender.
  4. Steam green beans for about 5 minutes, then sauté in butter with sliced almonds for 2 minutes until hot and coated.
