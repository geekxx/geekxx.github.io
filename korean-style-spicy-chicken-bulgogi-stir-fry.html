Korean-Style Spicy Chicken Bulgogi Stir-Fry

Spicy bulgogi-style chicken stir-fry with onions and bell peppers, served over rice.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs or breast, thinly sliced
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp gochujang (adjust to taste)
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar (optional)
  • 2 cups cooked white rice

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, combine gochujang, soy sauce, sesame oil, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, and rice vinegar. Add a splash of water if needed to thin the marinade.
  2. Toss sliced chicken in the marinade and refrigerate for at least 20 minutes (up to 2 hours).
  3. Heat a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add marinated chicken and cook for 5–6 minutes until mostly cooked.
  4. Add sliced onion and bell peppers and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until vegetables are tender-crisp and chicken reaches 165°F.
  5. Serve over warm white rice and garnish with sesame seeds if desired.
