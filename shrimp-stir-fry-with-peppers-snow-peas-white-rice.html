Shrimp Stir-Fry with Peppers, Snow Peas, White Rice

Author: Jeffrey Heinen

Quick shrimp stir-fry with bell pepper, snow peas, garlic, and white rice.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cup snow peas
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 cup white rice

Instructions

  1. Cook rice (1 cup rice + 1¼ cups water).
  2. Heat sesame oil in Instant Pot (sauté mode). Add garlic, peppers, snow peas, stir 2–3 min.
  3. Add shrimp and soy sauce. Cook until shrimp are pink (about 3 minutes). Serve over rice.
