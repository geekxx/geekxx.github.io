Mediterranean Baked Salmon

Salmon baked with cherry tomatoes, Kalamata olives, lemon, and roasted vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup sliced Kalamata olives
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup zucchini, cut into half-moons
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced

Instructions

  1. Toss zucchini and bell pepper with olive oil, salt, and pepper; roast at 400°F for 15–20 minutes.
  2. Place salmon on a parchment-lined baking sheet, scatter tomatoes, olives, and lemon slices on top.
  3. Bake at 400°F for 12–15 minutes until salmon flakes easily. Serve with roasted vegetables.
