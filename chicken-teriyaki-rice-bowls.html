Chicken Teriyaki Rice Bowls

Air-fried teriyaki chicken served over white rice with steamed vegetables and sesame seeds.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless skinless chicken thighs (about 8 oz each)
  • 1/4 cup teriyaki sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked white rice
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 cup sliced carrots
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook rice (1 cup uncooked rice + 1 1/4 cups water) per package instructions.
  2. Air fry chicken thighs at 375°F for 16–18 minutes, brushing with teriyaki sauce halfway through cooking.
  3. Steam broccoli and carrots for 4–5 minutes until tender.
  4. Slice chicken and serve over rice with vegetables. Garnish with sesame seeds if desired.
