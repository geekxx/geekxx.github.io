Spaghetti Squash with Turkey Italian Sausage

Roasted spaghetti squash tossed with Italian turkey sausage and marinara, served with roasted zucchini or steamed broccoli.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium spaghetti squash
  • 8 oz Italian turkey sausage (mild or spicy)
  • 2 cups marinara sauce (store-bought or homemade)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan (optional)
  • 1–2 cups roasted zucchini or steamed broccoli for the side

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Cut the spaghetti squash in half lengthwise and scoop out the seeds. Rub cut sides with a little olive oil and place cut-side down on a baking sheet. Roast 30–35 minutes until tender; let cool slightly, then shred with a fork into strands.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium heat. Add the turkey sausage (remove casing if necessary) and cook 6–8 minutes until browned. Add minced garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant. Stir in marinara and simmer 2–3 minutes.
  3. Toss cooked spaghetti squash strands with the sausage-marinara sauce. Portion as desired (about 5–6 oz sausage for one portion, full portion for another), and sprinkle with Parmesan if using.
  4. For the side: toss zucchini with olive oil, salt, and pepper and roast 12–15 minutes at 400°F, or steam broccoli 4–5 minutes.
