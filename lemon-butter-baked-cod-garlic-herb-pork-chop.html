Lemon-Butter Baked Cod & Garlic-Herb Pork Chop

A light seafood and pork plate: lemon-butter cod with a garlic-herb pork chop and roasted potatoes with sautéed spinach.

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets (6 oz each)
  • 1 pork chop (8 oz)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 lemon (juice + zest)
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Olive oil, salt, and pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss potatoes with oil, salt, and pepper and roast 25–30 minutes.
  2. Bake cod at 375°F for 12–15 minutes with lemon juice, zest, and a light butter drizzle.
  3. Pan-sear pork chop 3–4 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 145°F; rest 5 minutes.
  4. Sauté spinach with garlic in a bit of olive oil for 1–2 minutes and serve alongside.
