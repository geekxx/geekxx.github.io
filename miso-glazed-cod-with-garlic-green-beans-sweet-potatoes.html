Miso-Glazed Cod with Garlic Green Beans & Sweet Potatoes

Baked cod with a sweet-savory miso glaze, served with garlic green beans and roasted sweet potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 lb green beans
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Oil for roasting and sautéing

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss diced sweet potatoes with oil, salt, and pepper and roast for 25–30 minutes until tender.
  2. Whisk miso paste, honey, and soy sauce to make a glaze. Brush over cod fillets and bake at 400°F for 12–15 minutes until the fish flakes easily.
  3. Sauté green beans in a little oil with minced garlic for 4–5 minutes until tender-crisp.
