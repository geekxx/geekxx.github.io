Thai Green Curry with Jasmine Rice

Fragrant green curry with chicken (or shrimp/beef) and mixed vegetables, served with jasmine rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breasts, diced (or substitute shrimp or beef strips)
  • 2 tbsp green curry paste
  • 1 can (14 oz) light coconut milk
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (bell pepper, zucchini, snap peas)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Fish sauce or soy sauce to taste
  • 1 cup jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Cook jasmine rice according to package directions.
  2. In an Instant Pot or heavy saucepan, sauté the green curry paste in oil for 1 minute to bloom the flavors.
  3. Add coconut milk, diced chicken, and mixed vegetables. Simmer for about 10 minutes until chicken is cooked and vegetables are tender.
  4. Season to taste with fish sauce or soy sauce and serve over jasmine rice.
