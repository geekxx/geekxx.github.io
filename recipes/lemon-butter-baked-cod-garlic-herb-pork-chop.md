---

title: "Lemon-Butter Baked Cod & Garlic-Herb Pork Chop"
author: ""
description: "A light seafood and pork plate: lemon-butter cod with a garlic-herb pork chop and roasted potatoes with sautéed spinach."
prep_time: "10m"
cook_time: "30m"
total_time: "40m"
yield: "2 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "American"
ingredients:
  - "2 cod fillets (6 oz each)"
  - "1 pork chop (8 oz)"
  - "2 tbsp butter"
  - "1 lemon (juice + zest)"
  - "1 lb baby potatoes, halved"
  - "2 cups fresh spinach"
  - "1 clove garlic, minced"
  - "Olive oil, salt, and pepper"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss potatoes with oil, salt, and pepper and roast 25–30 minutes."
  - "Bake cod at 375°F for 12–15 minutes with lemon juice, zest, and a light butter drizzle."
  - "Pan-sear pork chop 3–4 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 145°F; rest 5 minutes."
  - "Sauté spinach with garlic in a bit of olive oil for 1–2 minutes and serve alongside."
