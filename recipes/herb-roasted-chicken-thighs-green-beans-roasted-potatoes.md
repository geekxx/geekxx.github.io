author: ""
description: "Juicy bone-in chicken thighs roasted with herbs, served with roasted potatoes and green beans almondine."
prep_time: "10m"
cook_time: "40m"
total_time: "50m"
yield: "4 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "American"
ingredients:
  - "4 bone-in chicken thighs"
  - "1 tsp dried thyme"
  - "1 tsp dried rosemary"
  - "1 tsp garlic powder"
  - "Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste"
  - "1 lb baby potatoes, halved"
  - "1 lb green beans, trimmed"
  - "1/4 cup sliced almonds"
  - "2 tbsp butter"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C)."
  - "Season chicken thighs with thyme, rosemary, garlic powder, salt, and pepper."
  - "Toss potatoes with oil, salt, and pepper and place in the roasting pan alongside chicken. Roast for 35–40 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F and potatoes are fork-tender."
  - "Steam green beans for about 5 minutes, then sauté in butter with sliced almonds for 2 minutes until hot and coated."

A straightforward roasted chicken meal with easy sides.
