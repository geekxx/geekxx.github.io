title: "Korean Bulgogi Chicken with Lettuce Wraps, Kimchi, Pickles"
author: ""
description: "Marinated Korean-style chicken served in lettuce wraps with kimchi and pickles."
prep_time: "30m"
cook_time: "10m"
total_time: "40m"
yield: "4 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "Korean"
ingredients:
  - "1 lb chicken breast or thighs, thinly sliced"
  - "1/4 cup soy sauce"
  - "1 tbsp honey"
  - "1 tbsp sesame oil"
  - "2 garlic cloves, minced"
  - "1 tsp grated ginger"
  - "Lettuce leaves (butter or romaine)"
  - "Store-bought kimchi"
  - "Pickled cucumbers or quick pickles"
instructions:
  - "Marinate chicken for 30 minutes in soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger."
  - "Stir-fry or grill chicken over medium-high heat until cooked through and slightly caramelized, about 6-8 minutes."
  - "Serve chicken in lettuce leaves topped with kimchi and pickles."
---

Simple Korean-style bulgogi chicken wrapped in crisp lettuce with kimchi and pickles for acidity.
