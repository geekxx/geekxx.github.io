title: "Chicken Teriyaki Rice Bowls"
author: ""
description: "Air-fried teriyaki chicken served over white rice with steamed vegetables and sesame seeds."
prep_time: "10m"
cook_time: "20m"
total_time: "30m"
yield: "2 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "Japanese-inspired"
ingredients:
  - "2 boneless skinless chicken thighs (about 8 oz each)"
  - "1/4 cup teriyaki sauce"
  - "1 1/2 cups cooked white rice"
  - "1 cup broccoli florets"
  - "1 cup sliced carrots"
  - "1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)"
instructions:
  - "Cook rice (1 cup uncooked rice + 1 1/4 cups water) per package instructions."
  - "Air fry chicken thighs at 375°F for 16–18 minutes, brushing with teriyaki sauce halfway through cooking."
  - "Steam broccoli and carrots for 4–5 minutes until tender."
  - "Slice chicken and serve over rice with vegetables. Garnish with sesame seeds if desired."
