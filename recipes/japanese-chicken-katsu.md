---

title: "Japanese Chicken Katsu"
author: ""
description: "Crispy panko-breaded chicken, air-fried and served with rice, shredded cabbage, and tonkatsu sauce."
prep_time: "10m"
cook_time: "18m"
total_time: "28m"
yield: "2 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "Japanese"
ingredients:
  - "2 chicken breasts (6 oz each)"
  - "1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs"
  - "1 egg, beaten"
  - "1/4 cup flour"
  - "Cooking spray or light oil"
  - "1 cup shredded cabbage"
  - "1 cup cooked white rice"
  - "2 tbsp tonkatsu sauce"
instructions:
  - "Pound chicken to even thickness. Dredge in flour, dip in beaten egg, then coat with panko."
  - "Air fry at 400°F for 15–18 minutes until crispy and cooked through, flipping halfway if needed."
  - "Serve sliced over rice with shredded cabbage and drizzle with tonkatsu sauce."
