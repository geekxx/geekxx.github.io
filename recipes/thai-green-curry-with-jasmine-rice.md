layout: recipe
title: "Thai Green Curry with Jasmine Rice"
author: ""
description: "Fragrant green curry with chicken (or shrimp/beef) and mixed vegetables, served with jasmine rice."
prep_time: "10m"
cook_time: "15m"
total_time: "25m"
yield: "4 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "Thai"
ingredients:
  - "1 lb chicken breasts, diced (or substitute shrimp or beef strips)"
  - "2 tbsp green curry paste"
  - "1 can (14 oz) light coconut milk"
  - "2 cups mixed vegetables (bell pepper, zucchini, snap peas)"
  - "1 tbsp oil"
  - "Fish sauce or soy sauce to taste"
  - "1 cup jasmine rice"
instructions:
  - "Cook jasmine rice according to package directions."
  - "In an Instant Pot or heavy saucepan, sauté the green curry paste in oil for 1 minute to bloom the flavors."
  - "Add coconut milk, diced chicken, and mixed vegetables. Simmer for about 10 minutes until chicken is cooked and vegetables are tender."
  - "Season to taste with fish sauce or soy sauce and serve over jasmine rice."
---

A simple, aromatic green curry that can be made with your preferred protein.
