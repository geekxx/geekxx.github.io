---
layout: recipe
title: "Mediterranean Baked Salmon"
author: ""
description: "Salmon baked with cherry tomatoes, Kalamata olives, lemon, and roasted vegetables."
prep_time: "10m"
cook_time: "20m"
total_time: "30m"
yield: "2 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "Mediterranean"
ingredients:
  - "2 salmon fillets (6 oz each)"
  - "1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved"
  - "1/4 cup sliced Kalamata olives"
  - "1 tbsp olive oil"
  - "1 lemon, thinly sliced"
  - "1 cup zucchini, cut into half-moons"
  - "1 red bell pepper, sliced"
instructions:
  - "Toss zucchini and bell pepper with olive oil, salt, and pepper; roast at 400°F for 15–20 minutes."
  - "Place salmon on a parchment-lined baking sheet, scatter tomatoes, olives, and lemon slices on top."
  - "Bake at 400°F for 12–15 minutes until salmon flakes easily. Serve with roasted vegetables."
