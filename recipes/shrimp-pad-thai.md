---
layout: recipe
title: "Shrimp Pad Thai"
author: ""
description: "Quick pad thai with shrimp, rice noodles, bean sprouts, and a tangy sauce."
prep_time: "10m"
cook_time: "10m"
total_time: "20m"
yield: "2 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "Thai"
ingredients:
  - "10 oz shrimp, peeled and deveined"
  - "4 oz flat rice noodles"
  - "1/2 cup store-bought pad thai sauce"
  - "1 cup bean sprouts"
  - "2 green onions, sliced"
  - "1 egg, lightly beaten"
  - "1 lime, cut into wedges"
instructions:
  - "Cook rice noodles according to package instructions and drain."
  - "Sauté shrimp in a wok or large skillet for 2–3 minutes until pink; remove and set aside."
  - "Add beaten egg to the pan and scramble briefly, then add pad thai sauce and noodles."
  - "Toss shrimp back in with bean sprouts and green onions and cook 1–2 minutes. Serve with lime wedges."
