title: "Miso-Glazed Cod with Garlic Green Beans & Sweet Potatoes"
author: ""
description: "Baked cod with a sweet-savory miso glaze, served with garlic green beans and roasted sweet potatoes."
prep_time: "10m"
cook_time: "30m"
total_time: "40m"
yield: "2–4 servings"
category: "Main"
cuisine: "Japanese"
ingredients:
  - "2 cod fillets (6 oz each)"
  - "2 tbsp white miso paste"
  - "1 tbsp honey"
  - "1 tbsp soy sauce"
  - "1 lb green beans"
  - "2 medium sweet potatoes, diced"
  - "2 garlic cloves, minced"
  - "Oil for roasting and sautéing"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss diced sweet potatoes with oil, salt, and pepper and roast for 25–30 minutes until tender."
  - "Whisk miso paste, honey, and soy sauce to make a glaze. Brush over cod fillets and bake at 400°F for 12–15 minutes until the fish flakes easily."
  - "Sauté green beans in a little oil with minced garlic for 4–5 minutes until tender-crisp."
---

A balanced, umami-forward fish dinner with roasted sweet potatoes and garlic green beans.
