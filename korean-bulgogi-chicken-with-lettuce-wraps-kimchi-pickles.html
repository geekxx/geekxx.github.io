Korean Bulgogi Chicken with Lettuce Wraps, Kimchi, Pickles

Marinated Korean-style chicken served in lettuce wraps with kimchi and pickles.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast or thighs, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Lettuce leaves (butter or romaine)
  • Store-bought kimchi
  • Pickled cucumbers or quick pickles

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken for 30 minutes in soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger.
  2. Stir-fry or grill chicken over medium-high heat until cooked through and slightly caramelized, about 6-8 minutes.
  3. Serve chicken in lettuce leaves topped with kimchi and pickles.
