Japanese Chicken Katsu

Crispy panko-breaded chicken, air-fried and served with rice, shredded cabbage, and tonkatsu sauce.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts (6 oz each)
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • Cooking spray or light oil
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1 cup cooked white rice
  • 2 tbsp tonkatsu sauce

Instructions

  1. Pound chicken to even thickness. Dredge in flour, dip in beaten egg, then coat with panko.
  2. Air fry at 400°F for 15–18 minutes until crispy and cooked through, flipping halfway if needed.
  3. Serve sliced over rice with shredded cabbage and drizzle with tonkatsu sauce.
