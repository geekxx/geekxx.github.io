Lemon Herb Chicken & Ribeye Steak with Asparagus, Mashed Potatoes

Author: Jeffrey Heinen

A simple weeknight dinner: lemon-herb chicken and a seared ribeye with roasted asparagus and mashed potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 1 boneless chicken breast (8 oz)
  • 1 ribeye steak (8–10 oz)
  • 1 lemon (juice + zest)
  • 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Potatoes: Boil until tender (10–12 min). Mash with a splash of milk, salt, and pepper.
  2. Asparagus: Toss in olive oil, roast at 425°F or air fry for about 8 minutes until tender-crisp.
  3. Chicken: Season with lemon juice, zest, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Air fry at 375°F for about 18 minutes (or until internal temp reaches 165°F).
  4. Steak: Season with salt and pepper. Pan-sear 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare (adjust to preference), then rest before slicing.
