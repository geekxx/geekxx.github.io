Shrimp Pad Thai

Quick pad thai with shrimp, rice noodles, bean sprouts, and a tangy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 10 oz shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 oz flat rice noodles
  • 1/2 cup store-bought pad thai sauce
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 egg, lightly beaten
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Instructions

  1. Cook rice noodles according to package instructions and drain.
  2. Sauté shrimp in a wok or large skillet for 2–3 minutes until pink; remove and set aside.
  3. Add beaten egg to the pan and scramble briefly, then add pad thai sauce and noodles.
  4. Toss shrimp back in with bean sprouts and green onions and cook 1–2 minutes. Serve with lime wedges.
